# How your privacy is protected

Put the call on speaker. CallGuard turns on the microphone only when you tap Guard this call or Start speech check and allow access. You can see when the microphone is on. It stops when you tap Stop or I hung up, after 60 seconds of silence, or when you leave or switch away from the page. The sample call keeps the microphone off.

CallGuard checks calls and audio files on your device. It has no server that processes calls, and it does not use analytics or send your audio or words anywhere.

For a call, CallGuard keeps audio and words in temporary memory and clears them when the session ends. For a voice clip, tap Clear check or leave the clip checker to clear the app’s copy and words. Your original file stays where you saved it.

Your browser downloads the app from Vercel. First use of speech features may download speech files from the app and model files from Hugging Face. These downloads contain no call data. Once the app and speech files are cached, you can use them offline. Spoken warnings use an installed voice on your device when available.

Your browser saves your language, speech check model, family alert setting and any family number you enter. You can remove these by clearing CallGuard’s site data.

Family alerts start off. If you turn them on, Tell my family opens WhatsApp with a short prepared message and the number you entered, if any. You decide whether to press Send. WhatsApp receives no call audio, transcript or detection reasons.
